# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard (Power BI Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Super Store Sales Dashboard** built using Power BI to analyse business performance across multiple dimensions such as sales, profit, customers, regions, and trends.  

The dashboard helps stakeholders make **data-driven decisions** by providing clear insights into key business metrics and patterns.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/rmtanushree13/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/SuperStore_Sales_Dataset.csv">Dataset</a>

## 🎯 Key Business Questions (KPIs)

- What are the **total sales, profit, and number of orders**?
- Which **product categories and sub-categories** generate the most revenue?
- How do **monthly sales and profit trends** vary year-over-year (YoY)?
- Which **regions and states** contribute the highest sales?
- What is the **sales distribution across customer segments**?
- Which **shipping modes** are most frequently used?
- What are the **preferred payment methods**?
- How does **profit vary across locations**?
- What is the **average shipping duration**?
- What are the **short-term sales forecasts (next 15 days)**?

## ⚙️ Process

### 1. Data Collection
- Gathered sales data including orders, customer details, shipping, and financial records.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Removed missing and inconsistent values  
- Standardized categorical fields  
- Created calculated columns:
  - Profit
  - Year-over-Year Growth
  - Shipping Duration  

### 3. Data Modeling
- Built relationships between datasets  
- Optimized data model for performance  

### 4. Dashboard Development
- Designed interactive visualizations:
  - KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Orders, Ship Days)
  - Bar Charts (Category, Sub-category, State)
  - Pie/Donut Charts (Segment, Region, Payment Mode)
  - Line Charts (Monthly Sales & Profit YoY)
  - Map Visualization (Sales by State)

### 5. Forecasting
- Implemented time-series forecasting for **next 15 days of sales**

### 6. Insights & Validation
- Validated trends and ensured data accuracy

## 📈 Project Insights

- **Overall Performance:**
  - Total Sales: **1.6M**
  - Total Profit: **175K**
  - Total Orders: **22K**
  - Average Shipping Time: **4 days**

- **Top Categories:**
  - Office Supplies (Highest Sales)
  - Technology
  - Furniture  

- **Top Sub-Categories:**
  - Phones
  - Chairs
  - Binders  
- **Regional Performance:**
  - West Region leads with **33%** of total sales  
  - East and Central regions follow  

- **Customer Segments:**
  - Consumer Segment dominates with **48%**

   - **Payment Methods:**
  - Cash on Delivery (43%) is most preferred  
  - Online (35%) and Cards (22%) follow  
- **Shipping Mode:**
  - Standard Class is the most used  

- **Top States:**
  - California and New York generate the highest sales  

- **Trend Analysis:**
  - Sales and profit fluctuate monthly with seasonal peaks  

- **Forecast:**
  - Stable sales trend expected in the next 15 days
